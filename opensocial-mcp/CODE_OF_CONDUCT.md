# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, as members, contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:
- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the users whose accounts this software touches.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:
- harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- proposing features designed to spam, manipulate platforms, scrape restricted data or evade platform rules;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory or by contacting a maintainer directly. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.
