## 1. Companies and Other Business Entities Act [Chapter 24:31] (Act 4 of 2019, in force 13 Feb 2020)
Source: https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/2019/4/eng@2020-10-28

| # | Fact | Section | Tag |
|---|------|---------|-----|
| 1.1 | [STATUTE] A "private company" is one whose articles (a) restrict share transfers, (b) limit members to **50** (excluding current and certain former employees), (c) prohibit any invitation to the public to subscribe for shares/debentures. Joint holders count as one member. | s.85(1)-(2) | VERIFIED-primary |
| 1.2 | [STATUTE] Private company knowingly exceeding 50 members commits an offence (daily fine, level two); if it invites the public, prospectus-liability ss.108, 111-114 apply as if it were public; Registrar may issue a category 1 civil penalty order. | s.85(4)-(5) | VERIFIED-primary |
| 1.3 | [STATUTE] Private → public: company alters its articles to remove the s.85 restrictions; it ceases to be private on that date and within **one month** must drop "(Private)" from its name and lodge with the Registrar a **statement in lieu of prospectus** (Second Schedule), unless a prospectus complying with the Eighth Schedule is issued and registered under s.106 in that period. | s.86(1) | VERIFIED-primary |
| 1.4 | [STATUTE] Resolution type needed to alter articles for private→public conversion (expected: special resolution) | not confirmed | UNVERIFIED |
| 1.5 | [STATUTE] Public → private requires a special resolution AND confirmation by the High Court. | s.85(3) | VERIFIED-primary |
| 1.6 | [STATUTE] Directors: s.195 deals with directors and their functions; the minimum number (commonly cited as 3 for public, 1 for private) was NOT confirmed verbatim. | s.195(1) (location only) | UNVERIFIED (number) |
| 1.7 | [STATUTE] Company secretary: s.198 governs functions, qualifications and disqualifications of company secretary; whether mandatory for all/public companies not confirmed verbatim. | s.198 (location only) | UNVERIFIED (scope) |
| 1.8 | [STATUTE] Prospectus provisions sit in Chapter III Part II, ss.104-114 (incl. s.106 registration of prospectus, s.108 civil liability for misstatements, s.111 offer-for-sale document deemed prospectus, s.114 restrictions on offering shares). | ss.104-114 | VERIFIED-primary (section map) |

## 3. ZSE Listings Requirements — SI 134 of 2019
Source: https://veritaszim.net/sites/veritas_d/files/SI%202019-134%20%20-%20%20Zimbabwe%20Stock%20Exchange%20Listings%20Requirements%29%202019_0.pdf (214 pp.)

| # | Fact | Section | Tag |
|---|------|---------|-----|
| 3.1 | [EXCHANGE] Main board: subscribed capital of **US$10 million** (incl. reserves & intangibles; excl. minority interests and unsupported revaluations). | s.87 | VERIFIED-primary |
| 3.2 | [EXCHANGE] Satisfactory profit history for preceding **five** financial years, "if applicable". | s.87 | VERIFIED-primary |
| 3.3 | [EXCHANGE] At least **30%** of each equity class held by public (unless ZSE agrees otherwise); if a pre-IPO private placement occurred, at least **20%** of total issued shares must be offered to the public. | s.87 | VERIFIED-primary |
| 3.4 | [EXCHANGE] Minimum public shareholders: **300** (equity), 25 (preference), 10 (debentures). | s.87 | VERIFIED-primary |
| 3.5 | [EXCHANGE] Sponsoring broker must be appointed for applications requiring listing particulars, for Part XVII documents, after breaches, or when ZSE requires; may be securities dealers, banks, accountants, legal practitioners, investment advisors, etc. | ss.16-19 | VERIFIED-primary |
| 3.6 | [EXCHANGE] Half-year interim report within **3 months** after end of first six months. | s.35(1) | VERIFIED-primary |

## 6. RBZ — offshore listings
Source: https://www.rbz.co.zw/documents/Regulations_Acts/2026/Foreign_Exchange_Guidelines_FXD5_version_2._March_2026.pdf

## Key distinctions for scripts
- Statute (COBE Act): private company = ≤50 members, transfer restrictions, no public offers; convert by altering articles + Registrar filing.
- Exchange rules (ZSE SI 134/2019; VFEX SI 62/2026): capital, float, shareholder spread, sponsor, reporting deadlines.
- Approvals: SECZ (licensing), RBZ prior approval for offshore listing.
- Commercial practice (not law): choice of sponsor/advisor, pricing, bookbuilding — not researched.
