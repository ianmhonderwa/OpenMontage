**[8] Rules for foreign investors – Who can own Zimbabwean shares?** (148.6s)

A useful rule to know. Under the Reserve Bank's March 2026 foreign exchange guidelines, non resident investors can together own up to 49 percent of a company on the Z S E or FINSEC, with no single foreign investor above 15 percent. Zimbabweans in the diaspora are not limited. And a local company that wants to list on a foreign exchange needs prior Reserve Bank approval.

_On-screen source note: Source: RBZ Foreign Exchange Transactions Guidelines (March 2026), ss.4.2.8 and 4.2.18. Educational summary; check the current text._

**[9] The African perspective – What to take from this week** (173.9s)

So, the African picture. Johannesburg follows global metal prices and the dollar. Harare can move sharply on small volumes. The V F E X gives US dollar exposure, but individual shares can jump or drop a lot in a day. And always check the date on any price you read.
