# 1\. The plan

Six series, each in the stickman style of the reference video (round white heads, thin black stick bodies, warm detailed cartoon backgrounds). Every episode is 45–60 seconds, narrated, and built on the same five-beat formula so viewers learn to expect a laugh and a lesson, and come back for the next one.

## The five-beat episode formula

1\. Hook (0–3 s). A surprising first line that makes people stop scrolling, e.g. 'No money, no shoes, and the most annoying questions in history.'

2\. Story (3–40 s). The tale told in 5–6 quick, very visual beats. Every line must be drawable.

3\. Joke (somewhere in the middle). One modern, gentle laugh: a funny aside, a deadpan reaction or a 'worst alibi ever' line.

4\. Lesson (last 8 s). One clear, powerful takeaway in a single sentence.

5\. Tease and follow (last 3 s). Name the next episode: 'Next time: … Follow so you don't miss it\!'

## Release rhythm (keeps people following)

Daily posting: rotate the series across the week, e.g. Mon Bible · Tue Socrates · Wed Aesop · Thu Nasreddin · Fri Bible · Sat Anansi/Grimm · Sun Bible. At 2 episodes a day, 1,000 episodes is about 16 months of content.

Numbering: always put 'Ep 12' on screen and in the caption; series viewers binge in order.

Playlists: one per series; pin Episode 1 of each to your profile.

Recurring hosts: Socrates and Nasreddin are their own hosts; the Storyteller (round glasses, red scarf) hosts Bible, Aesop, Anansi and Grimm.

Engagement question: end some episodes with a question to comment on (e.g. 'What would you do with the invisible ring?').

## Visual style (same as the 90-second stickman script)

STYLE LINE (already inside every scene prompt): Style: 2D cartoon stick-figure animation still. Every character has a big, perfectly round, pure-white head with a thick, clean black outline and a simple, expressive cartoon face (large white eyes, solid black pupils, bold black eyebrows, clear mouth shape). Bodies, arms and legs are thin solid-black stick lines with round black hands and small oval black feet. Clothes, hair and hats are simple flat-colour shapes with black outlines. Detailed flat-colour, cel-shaded cartoon background with soft gradients and warm, motivated lighting. Vertical 9:16 frame. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark.

NEGATIVE PROMPT: photorealistic, photo, 3D render, CGI, realistic skin, skin-coloured head, human anatomy, anime, manga, chibi, missing outline, extra limbs, extra fingers, extra heads, deformed face, crossed eyes, blurry, low resolution, text, letters, words, numbers, captions, speech-bubble text, logo, watermark, signature, frame border, split panels

MOTION SUFFIX (add to the animation prompt): Smooth, simple 2D cartoon animation. Keep every face, outline, colour and proportion exactly as in the image. No morphing, no new characters, no text.
