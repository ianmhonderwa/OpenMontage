Total about 1,040 episodes in six series. IDs: BIB, SOC, AES, NAS, ANA, GRM + 3-digit number. ALREADY WRITTEN (Batch 01, delivered as Word/PDF): BIB-001 to BIB-008, SOC-001 to SOC-008, AES-001 to AES-004, NAS-001 to NAS-004.

## AES – Aesop in 60 Seconds (313 episodes)

Source: 'Three Hundred Aesop's Fables', tr. G. F. Townsend (1867), Project Gutenberg \#21, <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/21/21-h/21-h.htm> . Episode order = the book's order, EXCEPT that AES-001 to AES-004 were 'The Lion and the Mouse', 'The Wolf and the Lamb', 'The Father and His Sons', 'The Crow and the Pitcher'. From AES-005 on, go through the book from the start, skipping those four fables.

## NAS – Nasreddin (about 100 episodes)

Source: 'The Turkish Jester', tr. George Borrow (1884), Project Gutenberg \#16244, <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/16244/16244-h/16244-h.htm> . NAS-n = the n-th anecdote (paragraphs starting 'One day', 'The Cogia', etc.). NAS-001 to 004 done (the pulpit 'do you know what I will say', camels without wings, the stars over the city, the dream of nine coins). SKIP anecdotes about sex or anything crude or demeaning (e.g. those about his wife in bed, and the one describing his disciple by skin colour); renumber continuously.

## ANA – Anansi & African Tales (37 episodes)

Source: 'West African Folk-Tales', W. H. Barker & C. Sinclair (1917), Project Gutenberg \#66923, <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/66923/66923-h/66923-h.htm> . ANA-n = the n-th tale in the book's contents, in order.

## GRM – Grimm Stickman Tales (184 episodes)

Source: 'Household Tales', Brothers Grimm, tr. Margaret Hunt (1884), Project Gutenberg \#5314, <https://www.gutenberg.org/files/5314/5314-h/5314-h.htm> . GRM-n = Hunt tale number n (so GRM-015 = tale 15, Hansel and Grethel). SKIP these tale numbers entirely: 3, 31, 40, 44, 46, 47, 66, 80, 110, 117, 123, 138, 148, 154, 167, 168. Keep any violence off-screen.
